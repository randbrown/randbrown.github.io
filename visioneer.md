## Hey, you found the Visioneer.

If you're here, we probably met somewhere out there (a festival, a burn, a campsite, a porch) and I handed you a sticker. Hi again.

**Ramblin' Visioneer** is my festival name: the one I go by at music festivals and burns. The Sasquatch on the sticker is the Visioneer: a shaggy, friendly wanderer with glowing blue eyes. Part guide, part trickster, part witness, always out rambling.

### What's on the sticker

* **The Sasquatch**: the Visioneer, mid-stride. Mystical, a little goofy, never a monster.
* **The angel and Mahakala**: two guardians keeping watch on either side, one gentle and one fierce.
* **The eye**: the watcher up top, for protection.
* **Flowers**: lilies, irises, and wildflowers from the garden at home.
* **The cosmos**: constellations, a spiral galaxy, atoms. The ramble goes a long way out.
